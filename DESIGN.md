## Brand & Style

This design system targets prospective cadets and candidates for competitive military, police, and civil service academies (AKMIL, AKPOL, Bintara, and Sekolah Kedinasan), as well as their invested parents. The emotional experience balances unyielding military discipline, high-tier academic prestige, and unwavering operational trust. It deliberately rejects casual ed-tech frivolity and aggressive tactical aesthetics in favor of a polished, officer-level academy atmosphere.

The style blends **Modern Corporate** with **Regal High-Contrast Accents**:
- Deep, solemn navy slate foundations establish authority, stability, and institutional stature without feeling dated.
- Rich gold metallic accents spotlight pivotal metrics, high-tier offerings, and directional calls to action.
- Razor-sharp structural alignment and high-contrast typography evoke precision, discipline, and regimented order.
- Crisp ribbons of patriotic red serve exclusively as precise structural delimiters and status markers, avoiding gratuitous decorative sprawl.
- Transparent, rigorously formatted placeholders (`.todo`) enforce uncompromising institutional integrity by visually segregating certified metrics from prospective data.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default commanding dark environment, built to evoke late-evening tactical briefing rooms and pristine naval academy ceremonies.

- **Primary (`#0B1B3A`) & Deep Neutrals (`#070F22`, `#050B18`)**: Dominates 80% of canvas volume. Deep navy grounds cards, hero panels, and sticky navigational chrome.
- **Secondary / Accent Gold (`#C9A227`)**: The hallmark of prestige and honor. Reserved for prominent actions, critical qualification figures, premium track borders, and directional focus indicators.
- **Tertiary / Insignia Red (`#C8102E`)**: Applied with strict discipline as razor-thin structural dividers, urgent registration notices, and categorical badge borders. It must never coat expansive surfaces.
- **Text & Contrast (`#FFFFFF`, `#BAC7DC`, `#6E82A5`)**: Pure white is allocated strictly to primary headers, tactical labels, and pivotal stats. Slate tints carry sustained structural reading to prevent visual strain.
- **Verification Token (`.todo`)**: Uses a distinct golden phosphor outline over a dark amber bedrock (`#1D1908`) to overtly demarcate pending client inputs during editorial drafts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear command presence:
- **Headlines & Display (Barlow Condensed)**: Engineered for authoritative presence, crisp horizontal compactness, and high-impact scanning. Rendered predominantly in uppercase with tracked spacing (`0.02em` to `0.08em`) to convey regimented discipline and administrative prestige.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Inter)**: Clean, objective, and neutral. Provides superior legibility across physical fitness metrics, academic curricula tables, and complex FAQ entries.
- **Numbers & Statistics**: Large metrics leverage bold condensed figures in gold to anchor immediate credibility without unverified motion trickery.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored around a **fixed-width 1152px containment boundary** (`max-w-6xl`), maintaining structured readability and preventing loose, unmoored eye travel across wide viewports.

- **Vertical Cadence**: Landing page panels maintain breathing room via strict rhythmic standards: mobile uses `py-16` (`space-3xl`, 4rem) while desktop applies `py-24` (`space-4xl`, 6rem). This separation creates an unhurried, distinguished cadence.
- **Grid Structure**:
  - Desktop: 12-column dynamic framework with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
  - Track Cards (AKMIL/AKPOL/Bintara/Kedinasan): 4-column balanced row on desktop (span 3), reflowing to 2x2 on tablet, and single-column stacked on mobile.
  - Programs & Packages: 3-column equal-height layout on desktop, stacked on mobile.
  - Verification Statistics: 4-up inline layout on desktop, condensing to a symmetrical 2x2 grid on mobile viewports.
- **Touch Target Floor**: Interactive components maintain a strict vertical minimum of 48px to support reliable single-handed touch accessibility.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layering and low-contrast architectural borders** rather than soft, floaty drop shadows. Academy aesthetics require firm physical presence and grounding:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - Canvas Base: `#070F22` (deepest matte tone).
  - Component Base / Panels: `#0B1B3A` (solid academy navy).
  - Hover / Elevated Surfaces: `#0E2246` (illuminated navy surface).
- **Architectural Borders**: Depth is reinforced through `1px` structural borders (`#1E3A6D`) that define clear edges between components and the dark background.
- **Prestige Glow**: Premium package tiers and hero actions employ an intentional ambient gold rim (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.15)`) coupled with a crisp `#C9A227` border, replacing arbitrary blur shadows with an authentic honor medal sheen.
- **Glassmorphic Navigation**: The sticky header employs `#0B1B3A` at 90% opacity paired with a `12px` backdrop blur and a crisp `#1E3A6D` bottom border.

## Shapes

This design system uses **Level 1 (Soft)** corner geometry:
- Standard components, interactive buttons, inputs, and cards employ a crisp, controlled `4px` (`rounded`) to `8px` (`rounded-lg`) corner radius.
- Circular elements are limited to instructor portrait crops, bullet checkmarks, and the floating WhatsApp beacon.
- Sharp, squared profiles reinforced with precise `1px` hairline boundaries underscore military discipline, structural rigor, and steadfast reliability, avoiding playful consumer-app roundness.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Gold Honor)**: Minimum 48px height, uppercase Barlow Condensed font (`label-caps`), solid `#C9A227` background, `#070F22` bold text. On hover: shifts to `#B38F1E` with subtle ambient gold glow.
- **Secondary Action (Cadet Outline)**: Minimum 48px height, 1px `#BAC7DC` or `#1E3A6D` border, transparent background, `#FFFFFF` text. On hover: background shifts to `#0E2246` and border transitions to `#C9A227`.
- **Floating WhatsApp Anchor**: Fixed bottom-right at 56x56px, circular, `#25D366` surface with clean white SVG icon. Padded cleanly against mobile safe-area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`).

### 2. Cards & Tracks
- **Track Selection Card**: Deep navy background (`#0B1B3A`), 1px outline (`#1E3A6D`). Features track insignia placeholder, uppercase condensed title, concise descriptor, and structured checklist tags with golden checks.
- **Premium Package Tier**: Accented with an active `#C9A227` top border or full outline, elevated surface `#0E2246`, and prominent gold button focus.

### 3. Verification & Draft Token (`.todo`)
- **Visual Design**: 1.5px dashed border in `#C9A227` against an amber-tinted background (`#1D1908`). 
- **Indicator**: Prepends a mini label reading `ISI DATA KLIEN` in 10px uppercase bold gold type. Protects institutional integrity by preventing fabricated statistics or pricing from masquerading as verified facts.

### 4. Patriotic Ribbon Dividers
- Dual-hairline or singular 2px strip in `#C8102E` accompanied by crisp white borders (`#FFFFFF`), used sparingly between landmark transitions (e.g., above closing CTA panels) to evoke national academy colors without overwhelming content readability.

### 5. Accordion (FAQ)
- Built on native `<details>` and `<summary>` elements. Navy-surface background (`#0B1B3A`), 1px border (`#1E3A6D`), custom SVG chevron that rotates on open. Clean, accessible, zero-dependency execution.
